About

My name is Grace, and I am an undergraduate student at the University of Florida majoring in Computer Science with a minor in Statistics. I am also pursuing certificates in Artificial Intelligence and Engineering Project Management.

I am passionate about building intelligent systems and exploring the cutting edge of AI. Currently, I am interning at Walmart Global Technology, where I work with agentic systems and retrieval-augmented generation (RAG), applying these technologies to solve real-world problems. I enjoy designing and implementing AI-driven solutions, full-stack web applications, and interactive visualizations. My focus is on RAG and creating intuitive interfaces.

Beyond academics and work, I am one of the directors of UF's WiNGHacks, the primary women and inclusive gender hackathon. I am incredibly excited about the upcoming year and the opportunity to foster a more inclusive community in tech, while inspiring the next generation of builders and innovators! Outside of that, I enjoy working out and cooking.
